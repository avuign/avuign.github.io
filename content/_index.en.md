# Antoine Vuignier

<img src="/images/profile.jpeg" alt="Antoine Vuignier" style="width: 210px; border-radius: 50%; float: right; margin-left: 20px;">


I'm a final-year PhD candidate in theoretical physics at [EPFL](https://www.epfl.ch/en/), with a defence expected in summer 2026. I am working on quantum field theory and quantum gravity. I turn abstract questions into precise mathematical problems and solve them with a combination of pen-and-paper calculations and computational workflows (numerical solvers, semidefinite programming) in Python and Mathematica. 

I am transitioning toward machine learning research,
drawn in particular by questions about the mathematical structure underlying learning and generalisation, and by the
prospect of developing methods that can genuinely accelerate scientific discovery.
