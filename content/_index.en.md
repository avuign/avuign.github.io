# Antoine Vuignier

<img src="/images/profile.jpeg" alt="Antoine Vuignier" style="width: 210px; border-radius: 50%; float: right; margin-left: 20px;">


I'm a final-year PhD candidate in theoretical physics at [EPFL](https://www.epfl.ch/en/) working on quantum field theory and quantum gravity. I turn abstract questions into precise mathematical problems and solve them with a combination of pen-and-paper calculations and computational workflows (numerical solvers, semidefinite programming) in Python and Mathematica. I've published 5 papers in high-energy theoretical physics. Recently, I've started implementing neural networks to solve variational problems from my own research.

I'm now looking for a research position where strong mathematical thinking is central, the problems require creativity, and the outcome has applications I can witness. I’m based in Valais, Switzerland, and expect to defend my thesis during the summer of 2026.
